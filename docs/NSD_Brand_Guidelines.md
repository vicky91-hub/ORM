# North Star Digital — Brand Guide (Reference)
*Source: NSD_Brand_Guidelines.pptx, issued April 2026 · For internal use on ORM/NSD dashboards, decks, and reports*

## 1. Brand Foundation
- **Archetype:** The Sage — clarity, precision, expertise. Authority without arrogance. Research-led, data-first.
- **Tone of voice:** Confident, never boastful. Precise, never cold. Insightful, never obvious. Direct, always respectful.
- **Reference peers:** McKinsey (authority), BCG (structured intelligence), Apple (restraint), EY/Deloitte (trust).

## 2. Logo System
- **On white:** Obsidian (#0A0A0A) — primary usage.
- **On dark:** White (#FFFFFF) — premium usage.
- **On gold:** Accent usage only.
- Star is part of the N mark and always matches its color; the two must never be separated.
- **Minimum size:** Print 30mm (1.18") · Desktop 120px width · Mobile 80px width · Favicon 32px (full logo).
- **Clear space:** Equal to the height of the N mark on all sides.
- **Never:** apply gradients/shadows, place on busy photo backgrounds, use Gold on white (low contrast), rotate/stretch/distort, add outlines or strokes.

### Tier III — Signature Accent (max 10–15% coverage)
| Name | Hex |
|---|---|

| Deep Gold | `#B8923A` |

### Tier IV — Functional Neutrals
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dusk | `#4A4E69` | Secondary text on dark |
| Fog | `#7F8C8D` | Captions, metadata |
| Mist | `#F5F6FA` | Card backgrounds, panels |
| Cloud | `#ECECEC` | Dividers, borders |

### 60 / 30 / 10 Rule (applies to every post/dashboard panel)
- **60% Neutral** — Arctic White, Mist, or Constellation (foundation)
- **30% Primary** — Atlas, Meridian (structure, text, charts, data)
- **10% Accent** — Compass Gold (section numbers, icons, callouts)

### Forbidden Pairings
- Gold text on white background (fails contrast/accessibility)
- Meridian + Constellation both as heavy fills (no hierarchy)
- Mist background + Fog text (insufficient contrast)
- Pure `#FFFFFF` + `#000000` only (too stark, breaks brand warmth)
- Any bright/saturated colors (red, green, orange) — breaks the palette

## 4. Typography
Default/fallback font for all social graphics, documents, decks and **dashboards: Arial** (universally available).

| Role | Brand Font | Fallback | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Cormorant Garamond Light | Arial Black | 56px / 36–44pt | Hero headlines, cover titles |
| Heading 1 | Cormorant Garamond Semibold | Arial Bold | 40px / 28–32pt | Section titles, slide headlines |
| Heading 2 | Lato Bold UPPERCASE | Arial Bold UPPERCASE | 22px / 18–20pt | Sub-section labels, categories |
| Body | Lato Light | Arial Regular | 16px / 14–16pt | Body copy, descriptions |
| Caption | Lato Regular UPPERCASE | Arial Regular | 12px / 10–12pt | Metadata, sources, footnotes |

Titles: 36–44pt Bold · Body: 14–16pt Regular · Captions: 10–12pt · Stat numbers: 48–60pt Bold

## 5. Layout & Format Rules (adapt for dashboard cards/panels)
- **Sandwich structure:** dark cover → light body → dark close.
- Leave **30–40% white space** on every panel/post.
- Keep clear type hierarchy — display vs. body should never be the same size.
- Max 4 colors in a single graphic or chart.
- No more than 1 stat callout per "story"-style frame.
- Always cite data sources when displaying statistics.
- Include domain/attribution (`thenorthstardigital.ai`) where relevant on external-facing exports.

## 6. Tone & Voice (for report/dashboard copy, alerts, summaries)
- **Lead with insight:** open with the data point, not the methodology (e.g., "72.3% of..." not "We surveyed...").
- **Be precise:** use exact numbers, avoid vague qualifiers ("many," "most") when data exists.
- **Stay direct:** short sentences for impact; longer only for nuance. No filler.
- **Attribution:** always cite source and methodology when showing data; on-graphic credit line "Source: NSD Research, [Year]" or "Consumer Research by Northstar Digital."

## 7. Do's & Don'ts Summary
**Always**
- Constellation (`#1A1F36`) for cover/title cards and dark sections
- Gold accent kept under 10–15% of frame
- Clear hierarchy between display and body text
- Meridian (`#1A5276`) as primary chart/stat color
- Arctic White (`#F8F9FA`) — never pure white — for light backgrounds
- Cite data sources wherever statistics appear

**Never**
- Gold as a large background fill (accent only)
- Gold text on white (low contrast)
- More than 4 colors in one graphic/chart
- Gradients/shadows/effects on the logo
- Meridian + Constellation as equally heavy fills
- Bright/saturated colors (red, green, orange)
- Publish data without citing the source

## Application notes for ORM / dashboard work
When building ORM dashboards (Social, GMB, Reddit, Quora) or any NSD-branded deliverable:
- Use **Constellation** for header/nav bars, **Arctic White/Mist** for content backgrounds, **Meridian** for primary charts/KPIs, **Compass Gold** sparingly for highlights/alerts only.
- Sentiment charts: keep to Meridian (positive/primary), Atlas (neutral/secondary), and a single muted tone for negative — avoid red/green traffic-light colors per the "no bright/saturated" rule; if a red/green convention is required for usability (e.g., sentiment status), flag it as an intentional exception.
- Typography: Arial across the dashboard (brand default for digital/dashboard contexts).
- Maintain the 60/30/10 balance across dashboard panels; don't over-use Gold.
